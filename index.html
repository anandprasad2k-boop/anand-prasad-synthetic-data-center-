05 — Physical plausibility

Do predictions stay within a realistic PUE envelope?

For each prediction, we compute the PUE implied by predicted cooling ÷ predicted IT load, and flag any that fall outside a physically realistic range. Neither architecture should predict impossible electrical/cooling combinations.
